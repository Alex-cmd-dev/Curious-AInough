# 🧠 1. AI and Large Language Models


## The kind of AI people usually mean

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a broad term for computer systems that do tasks such as recognizing images, understanding speech, or generating language. In everyday conversation, “AI” often means a chat assistant powered by a large language model (LLM), such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok.

An LLM learns patterns from data and uses them to generate responses from your prompt and the other context it receives. The assistant is the product you interact with; the LLM is one kind of model that can power it. They are often treated as the same thing in everyday conversation, but they are different: AI includes systems that are not LLMs, and an assistant can combine an LLM with tools such as web search, file analysis, or image generation.

LLMs can be useful for explaining, drafting, learning, and exploring ideas. They can also produce confident mistakes, so check important claims and make your own decisions. This guide focuses mostly on using LLM-powered assistants well.

## Choosing an assistant

There is no single best provider for every task. The products change often, and features depend on the model, plan, region, and settings. Try a few with the same task and compare the results, sources, ease of use, and cost. Check the current product details before choosing one for regular work.

| Provider | Where it may be a good fit | Trade-offs to consider |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OpenAI (ChatGPT)](https://chatgpt.com/overview/) | A broad assistant experience with tools for tasks such as working with files, data, images, and voice. | Available models, tools, and usage limits vary by plan and may change. Check which model and tools your account actually has. |
| [Anthropic (Claude)](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) | A useful option to compare for writing, coding, and working through long documents. Anthropic publishes model cards describing evaluations and safeguards. | Some features and model access depend on plan or API; safeguards may limit certain requests. Verify important outputs just as you would with any assistant. |
| [Google (Gemini)](https://gemini.google/overview/) | Worth considering for multimodal tasks and workflows connected to Google's products. Gemini models support different combinations of text, image, audio, and other inputs. | Features and integrations vary by account, subscription, and region. A Google connection is convenient only if it fits your workflow and privacy needs. |
| [xAI (Grok)](https://grok.com/) | May suit people who want an assistant connected to X and its public conversations, where that feature is available. | Social posts can be unverified, biased, or misleading; access and features vary. Treat a fast or current answer as a lead to check, not proof. |

These are practical starting points, not rankings or guarantees. A provider's feature list describes what its product can do, not how well it will do your particular task. Compare answers against reliable sources, and review each provider's data controls before sharing sensitive information.
