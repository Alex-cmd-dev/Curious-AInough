# Activity: Plan a Meal From What You Have

[⬅ Back to Activities](README.md)

## The scenario

You want dinner tonight. You have pasta, a can of tomatoes, an onion, and frozen peas. You have 30 minutes and one pot and one pan. Use the five-step loop to choose a meal you can actually make.

## Before you ask

Write down your goal and one thing you will check yourself, such as whether the ingredients are still usable. You can substitute your own pantry items, but avoid sharing private information.

## Copy this into your AI assistant

```text
I want to make a simple dinner tonight. I have pasta, a can of tomatoes, an onion, and frozen peas. I have 30 minutes, one pot, and one pan.

Help me use this five-step loop. First, restate my goal and the context I gave you. Then suggest two possible meals with short steps and identify anything you need to know or that I should check in my kitchen. Do not assume I have other ingredients or equipment. Finally, ask me to choose or revise a plan; do not choose for me.
```

## Do the activity

1. **Define the goal:** Compare the assistant's restatement with the goal you wrote.
2. **Give context:** Correct any missing detail that changes what is possible.
3. **Explore:** Compare the two options. Ask why a step is needed if you do not understand it.
4. **Check:** Look at your actual ingredients, equipment, and time. Check any cooking instruction that seems unclear before using it.
5. **Decide:** Pick, change, or reject an option and write one sentence explaining why.

## Check your understanding

Which part of your decision came from AI, and which part required your own observation or judgment? Could you explain why your chosen meal fits your goal?
