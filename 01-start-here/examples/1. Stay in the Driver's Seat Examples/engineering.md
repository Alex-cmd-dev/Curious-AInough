# Engineering: Stay in the Driver's Seat

[Back to examples](README.md)

**Remember:** You set the goal. AI assists. You decide.

## Goal
Build a popsicle-stick bridge that holds the most weight.

## The five-step loop
1. **Define the goal:** Build a popsicle-stick bridge that holds the most weight.
2. **Give context:** Tell AI the span, the number of sticks, the glue, and the contest rules.
3. **Explore:** Ask AI for three bridge designs and why each might be strong or weak.
4. **Check:** Check the rules, test a small version, and see where it breaks. Look up any strength claim from a reliable source.
5. **Decide:** Pick the design, build it, and record what you learned from the test.

## Why it fits engineering
Real designs need testing and review. AI gives ideas; testing gives proof.

## Try it yourself
Write one goal from your own classes or life. What will you check before you trust the answer?
