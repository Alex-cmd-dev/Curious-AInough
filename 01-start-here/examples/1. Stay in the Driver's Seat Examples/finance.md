# Finance: Stay in the Driver's Seat

[Back to examples](README.md)

**Remember:** You set the goal. AI assists. You decide.

## Goal
Save $600 for a spring break trip in 4 months.

## The five-step loop
1. **Define the goal:** Save $600 for a spring break trip in 4 months.
2. **Give context:** Share your monthly income, fixed bills, and spending habits. Leave out account numbers.
3. **Explore:** Ask AI for three savings plans and the trade-off of each (cut subscriptions, extra shifts, a smaller trip).
4. **Check:** Do the math yourself ($600 / 4 months = $150 a month). Check any savings rate on the bank's own site.
5. **Decide:** Choose the plan that fits your life. AI does not know what matters to you. This is not financial advice.

## Why it fits finance
Finance is about choices with money and time. AI can lay out options, but you weigh the risk.

## Try it yourself
Write one goal from your own classes or life. What will you check before you trust the answer?
