# Accounting: Stay in the Driver's Seat

[Back to examples](../README.md)

**Remember:** You set the goal. AI assists. You decide.

## Goal
Find out why my bank balance is $40 lower than my own spending notes.

## The five-step loop
1. **Define the goal:** Find out why my bank balance is $40 lower than my own spending notes.
2. **Give context:** Share a list of your purchases and dates. Hide account numbers.
3. **Explore:** Ask AI for possible reasons: a pending charge, a subscription, a forgotten purchase, or a duplicate.
4. **Check:** Match each item to your bank statement line by line. Add up the totals yourself.
5. **Decide:** Decide which charges are correct, dispute any error, and update your records.

## Why it fits accounting
Accounting is about records you can trust. AI can suggest where to look, but the statement is the evidence.

## Try it yourself
Write one goal from your own classes or life. What will you check before you trust the answer?
