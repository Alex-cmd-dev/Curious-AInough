# 📎 Examples: Start Here

[⬅ Back to Start Here](../README.md)

Applying the five-step loop (**define the goal → give context → explore → check → decide**) across different fields.

### 🔬 Science & Engineering

**Scenario:** Choosing a topic for a science fair project.

> [!TIP]
> "I need a science fair project I can finish in three weeks with supplies from home. I'm interested in plants and weather. Suggest three testable questions, what I'd measure for each, and what could go wrong."

### 📜 Humanities & History

**Scenario:** Picking a thesis for a short history paper.

> [!TIP]
> "I'm writing a 1,500-word paper on a turning point in the Civil Rights Movement. Suggest three possible arguments I could make, what primary sources I'd need for each, and which one is most debatable."

### 💼 Business & Entrepreneurship

**Scenario:** Deciding what to pitch for a class fundraiser.

> [!TIP]
> "My club needs to raise $500 in one month for a low-effort, low-risk fundraiser. Suggest three options, what each would take to run, and what I should check before committing."

### 🎨 Arts & Design

**Scenario:** Starting a new creative project with a deadline.

> [!TIP]
> "I have three weeks to make a short piece for a student art show, working in charcoal. Suggest three directions, what skills each would stretch, and what would make each one hard to finish in time."

### 🌍 Everyday Life & Community

**Scenario:** Planning a trip on a tight budget.

> [!TIP]
> "I have $300 and four days for a trip with two friends, all of us love hiking. Suggest three destinations within a day's drive, what each would cost, and what I should double-check before booking."
