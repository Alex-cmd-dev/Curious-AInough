# 📎 Examples: Brainstorming & Curiosity

[⬅ Back to Brainstorming & Curiosity](../README.md)

Applying "generate options, then challenge them" across different fields.

### 🔬 Science & Engineering

> [!TIP]
> "I want to test whether music affects plant growth for a science fair. Suggest three experiment designs, then challenge each one on cost, time, and what could confound the results."

### 📜 Humanities & History

> [!TIP]
> "I want to argue that a specific technology changed the outcome of a historical event. Suggest three technologies I could focus on, then argue against each choice: what's the counter-argument a historian would raise?"

### 💼 Business & Entrepreneurship

> [!TIP]
> "I want to start a small campus laundry pickup service. Suggest three pricing models, then challenge each one on cost, liability, and whether students would actually pay."

### 🎨 Arts & Design

> [!TIP]
> "I want to design a mural for my school's cafeteria. Suggest three themes, then challenge each one on cost of materials, how long it will hold up, and whether it fits a space everyone eats in."

### 🌍 Everyday Life & Community

> [!TIP]
> "I want to run a campus repair event. Suggest three formats, then challenge each one on space, volunteers, accessibility, and cost. Which questions should I answer before deciding?"
