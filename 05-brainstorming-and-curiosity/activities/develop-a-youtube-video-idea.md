# Activity: Develop a YouTube Video Idea

[⬅ Back to Activities](README.md)

## The scenario

You have a rough idea for a YouTube video. Use AI to explore ways to develop it and to challenge your assumptions, while keeping the idea and final decision yours.

## Before you ask

Write a one-sentence video idea in your own words. Add two criteria that matter to you, such as who the video is for, how long it might take to make, or what you want viewers to learn.

## Copy this into your AI assistant

```text
My starting YouTube video idea is: [write your idea here].
My intended audience is: [describe them briefly].
My criteria are: [list two things that matter to you].

Suggest three distinct directions I could take this idea. Do not write the script or pick a direction for me. For each direction, point out one assumption I may be making and one question I should investigate. Then make the strongest case against my original idea. End by asking what I want to keep, change, test, or discard.
```

## Do the activity

1. Compare the directions with the idea you wrote before asking AI. Notice which suggestions are genuinely different and which are just wording changes.
2. Choose one assumption or question worth examining. Ask a follow-up that helps you think it through rather than asking AI to decide.
3. Consider who might be affected by the video and what you would need to check before publishing claims, using someone else's work, or showing other people.
4. Decide what to keep, change, test, or discard using your own criteria. Name one small test, such as asking a potential viewer which version they would watch and why.

## Check your understanding

Which suggestion widened your thinking? Which criticism was useful, and what evidence or feedback could help you decide whether to act on it?
