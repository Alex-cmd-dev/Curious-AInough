# Finance: Give Good Directions

[Back to prompting examples](README.md)

**Remember:** You decide where you're going. A good prompt gives AI better directions.

## Before
```text
How do I save money?
```
**Why this falls short:** It does not say how much, by when, or from what income. The answer will be general advice.

## After
```text
My goal is: Save $600 for a spring break trip in 4 months.
Context you need: I earn about $400 a month from a part-time job. Rent and phone cost $250. I spend the rest on food, rides, and subscriptions.
Constraints: No risky investing. Keep it realistic for a student. This is for learning, not financial advice.
Please produce: Three savings plans with a monthly amount for each and the trade-off of each.
Help me check it by: Show the math so I can redo it, and tell me what you do not know about my situation.
```

| Part | What this prompt does |
|---|---|
| 🎯 Goal | Save $600 for a spring break trip in 4 months. |
| 📚 Context | I earn about $400 a month from a part-time job. Rent and phone cost $250. I spend the rest on food, rides, and subscriptions. |
| 🚧 Constraints | No risky investing. Keep it realistic for a student. This is for learning, not financial advice. |
| 📦 Output | Three savings plans with a monthly amount for each and the trade-off of each. |
| ✅ Check | Show the math so I can redo it, and tell me what you do not know about my situation. |

## Read and follow up
Read the answer first. What is unclear or missing? Then keep the conversation going, for example:
- "What if I get paid less one month?"
- "Which plan is easiest to stick with?"

## Why it fits finance
Finance is about choices with money and time. Giving numbers and limits lets AI compare real options instead of repeating tips.

## Try it yourself
Take a vague prompt you have used before. Rewrite it with the five parts above, then compare the answers.
