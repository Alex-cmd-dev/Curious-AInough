# 📎 Examples: Prompting & Context

[⬅ Back to Prompting & Context](../README.md)

Applying **Goal + Context + Constraints + Output + Check** across different fields.

### 🔬 Science & Engineering

**Vague version:** "Explain photosynthesis."

> [!TIP]
> "Help me understand photosynthesis for a first-year biology quiz. I know the basic equation but struggle with the light-dependent reactions. Explain them in five short steps, then ask me two questions. Flag any simplifications."

### 📜 Humanities & History

**Vague version:** "What caused World War I?"

> [!TIP]
> "I'm writing a high school essay on the causes of World War I and need to cover alliances, nationalism, and militarism in under 800 words. Explain how the three connect using one concrete example each, and tell me which claims are still debated by historians."

### 💼 Business & Entrepreneurship

**Vague version:** "Write a marketing plan."

> [!TIP]
> "I'm marketing a student-run tutoring service to first-year college students with a $0 budget, only social media and flyers. Draft a one-page plan with three channels, one action per channel, and one metric to check if it's working."

### 🎨 Arts & Design

**Vague version:** "How do I use color theory?"

> [!TIP]
> "I'm painting a poster for a jazz concert and want a warm, energetic palette using only three colors plus black and white. Suggest two palettes, explain why each fits the mood, and tell me what could make the poster hard to read from a distance."

### 🌍 Everyday Life & Community

**Vague version:** "Give me a meal plan."

> [!TIP]
> "Plan five dinners for one person, each under 30 minutes and $8, no shellfish (allergy). Give me a shopping list organized by store aisle, and flag any recipe that reuses an ingredient I'd otherwise waste."
