# Activity: Check a Cooking Claim

[⬅ Back to Activities](README.md)

## The scenario

Someone says, “Adding oil to pasta water prevents the pasta from sticking.” Use AI to plan a search, then inspect the evidence yourself. The aim is to practice verification, not to collect a confident answer.

## Before you ask

Write down what you currently think and what evidence might change your mind.

## Copy this into your AI assistant

Copy the whole activity prompt below and paste it into the AI provider you already use. Stay in that chat to complete the activity and reflection.

```text
I want to practice checking a claim instead of relying on a confident answer. First ask what I currently think about this claim and what evidence might change my mind: “Adding oil to pasta water prevents the pasta from sticking.” Then help me investigate it. Do not give me a verdict yet. Suggest three useful search terms, two questions that would help test the claim, and types of sources I could inspect. If you can provide links, label them as leads I must open and check myself. Do not invent a source, quote, or experiment. Wait while I search and open at least two sources myself. Then ask me to report who made each source, what it says or demonstrates, and whether it supports the specific claim. Help me compare preventing pasta from sticking during cooking with changing it after draining. Ask me to write a two-sentence conclusion with links and one remaining uncertainty; then challenge my conclusion without treating new claims as facts. Finish by asking whether I can identify the exact evidence for my conclusion and what I would do if sources disagree or are too weak. After the reflection, ask whether I would like a similar claim-checking exercise. If I say yes, choose a practical claim, do not reveal a verdict upfront, do not invent evidence, and have me inspect sources directly before drawing a conclusion.
```

## Do the activity

1. Search using one suggested term. Open at least two sources yourself; do not rely on an AI summary or search snippet.
2. For each source, record who made it, what it actually says or demonstrates, and whether it supports the specific claim.
3. Compare the sources. Note any difference between preventing sticking during cooking and changing the pasta after draining.
4. Write a two-sentence conclusion with links to the sources you opened and one remaining uncertainty. Ask the assistant to challenge your conclusion, then check any new claim it makes.

## Check your understanding

Can you point to the exact part of each opened source that supports your conclusion? What would you say if the sources disagree or are too weak to settle the question?
