# 📎 Examples: Research & Verification

[⬅ Back to Research & Verification](../README.md)

Applying the verification checklist across different fields.

### 🔬 Science & Engineering

**Scenario:** An AI answer claims a specific chemical reaction is used industrially at large scale.

> [!TIP]
> "List every factual claim in this answer about industrial ammonia production, and tell me what kind of primary source (patent, industry report, peer-reviewed paper) would verify each one."

### 📜 Humanities & History

**Scenario:** An AI answer attributes a quote to a historical figure.

> [!TIP]
> "This answer quotes Lincoln directly. Tell me how I would confirm this quote is real: what archive, collection, or verified source should I check, and what are signs a historical quote might be misattributed?"

### 💼 Business & Entrepreneurship

**Scenario:** An AI answer cites a market-size statistic for a business plan.

> [!TIP]
> "This answer says the market is worth $4 billion. Tell me what kind of source would actually support that number, and what questions I should ask about how that figure was calculated before I put it in a pitch deck."

### 🎨 Arts & Design

**Scenario:** An AI answer claims a painting technique originated with a specific artist or movement.

> [!TIP]
> "This answer credits this technique to a specific art movement. What would I need to check, museum records, art historians, dated works, to confirm that attribution instead of a popular myth?"

### 🌍 Everyday Life & Community

**Scenario:** An AI answer recommends a home remedy or health claim.

> [!TIP]
> "This answer makes a health claim about a home remedy. Tell me what it would take to verify this: what kind of study, medical source, or professional I should check with before trying it or sharing it with someone else."
