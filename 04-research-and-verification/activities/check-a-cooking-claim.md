# Activity: Check a Cooking Claim

[⬅ Back to Activities](README.md)

## The scenario

Someone says, “Adding oil to pasta water prevents the pasta from sticking.” Use AI to plan a search, then inspect the evidence yourself. The aim is to practice verification, not to collect a confident answer.

## Before you ask

Write down what you currently think and what evidence might change your mind.

## Copy this into your AI assistant

```text
I want to investigate this claim: “Adding oil to pasta water prevents the pasta from sticking.” Do not give me a verdict yet. Suggest three useful search terms, two questions that would help test the claim, and types of sources I could inspect. If you can provide links, label them as leads I must open and check myself. Do not invent a source, quote, or experiment.
```

## Do the activity

1. Search using one suggested term. Open at least two sources yourself; do not rely on an AI summary or search snippet.
2. For each source, record who made it, what it actually says or demonstrates, and whether it supports the specific claim.
3. Compare the sources. Note any difference between preventing sticking during cooking and changing the pasta after draining.
4. Write a two-sentence conclusion with links to the sources you opened and one remaining uncertainty. Ask the assistant to challenge your conclusion, then check any new claim it makes.

## Check your understanding

Can you point to the exact part of each opened source that supports your conclusion? What would you say if the sources disagree or are too weak to settle the question?
