# 📎 Examples: Ethics, Privacy & Security

[⬅ Back to Ethics, Privacy & Security](../README.md)

Applying "check before you share, check before you rely on it" across different fields.

### 🔬 Science & Engineering

**Scenario:** Using AI to help analyze survey data from a school research project.

> [!TIP]
> "Before I paste this survey dataset into an AI tool, list anything that could identify a specific participant, name, ID number, exact birthdate, free-text answers, and tell me what I should remove or anonymize first."

### 📜 Humanities & History

**Scenario:** Quoting a family member's private interview or letters in a history project.

> [!TIP]
> "I want to use excerpts from my grandmother's letters in a class project. What should I think about regarding her privacy and consent, even though she's a family member, before I share or publish this?"

### 💼 Business & Entrepreneurship

**Scenario:** Using AI to help analyze a customer list for a student business.

> [!TIP]
> "Before I upload this customer spreadsheet to an AI tool, list anything that looks like a name, email, phone number, or payment detail, and tell me what I should remove or replace with placeholders first."

### 🎨 Arts & Design

**Scenario:** Using someone else's photo or voice as reference material for a creative project.

> [!TIP]
> "I want to use a photo of a classmate as reference for a portrait I plan to post online. What should I ask them for, and what should I consider about consent and attribution before I share the finished piece?"

### 🌍 Everyday Life & Community

**Scenario:** Getting help drafting a message about a family health situation.

> [!TIP]
> "Before I paste this message into an AI tool, list anything that looks like a password, account number, personal record, health information, or confidential school/workplace data, and tell me what I should remove or redact first."
