## Try it

> [!TIP]
> Before installing a new MCP server, skill, or extension, walk through this checklist:
>
> 1. Who publishes this, and is that source trustworthy?
> 2. What can it actually see (files, accounts, the internet)?
> 3. What can it actually do (read only, or also write/send/delete)?
> 4. Does it need all of that access for what I want it to do?
> 5. Would I be comfortable if it failed or misbehaved on my most sensitive file?
>
> If you can't answer all five, don't connect it to anything sensitive yet.
