# Challenge: Learn Photosynthesis With AI

[⬅ Back to Examples](README.md)

## The scenario

You are starting a biology unit on photosynthesis. You know that plants use sunlight, but you are unsure what happens to the matter and energy along the way. Use AI as a tutor to build your understanding, not to write an assignment for you.

## Try the challenge

1. **Start with your own idea.** Without using AI, explain what you think a plant takes in and what it produces during photosynthesis. Include what you think happens to the sunlight.
2. **Ask for coaching.** Try this prompt: “I’m a first-year biology student learning photosynthesis. Ask me one question at a time to help me understand where the atoms in glucose come from and what role light plays. Don’t give me a full lecture or answer my questions for me.”
3. **Check your understanding.** Ask the AI to help you trace the carbon atoms from carbon dioxide into a sugar molecule. Then ask what happens to the oxygen atoms in the carbon dioxide and water. If the answers conflict with your class notes or textbook, trust the course source and ask your instructor about the difference.
4. **Explain it back.** Close the chat and explain photosynthesis in your own words, including what goes in, what comes out, and how light energy is involved.
5. **Try a new question.** Without AI, answer: “If a plant is kept in darkness for a long time but still has water and carbon dioxide, can it keep making sugar by photosynthesis? Explain why.” Afterward, ask AI to critique your reasoning and check its feedback against your notes.

## Check your understanding

You should be able to describe the inputs and outputs, explain that matter is rearranged into sugars and oxygen, and distinguish the role of light energy from the atoms that make up the products. If you get stuck, return to the part you cannot explain and ask for one hint at a time.

Follow your course's AI-use rules. The goal is to practice explaining biology in your own words rather than relying on AI-written explanations.
