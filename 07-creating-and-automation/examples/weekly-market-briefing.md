# 📈 Example: Automate a Weekly Market Briefing

[⬅ Back to Examples](README.md)

**Recurring problem:** Keeping up with U.S. stocks and the coming week's economic events takes time every Sunday.

**Automated workflow:** On a weekly schedule, an AI assistant gathers current, accessible market information, drafts the same short briefing format, and flags anything it could not verify. A person reviews the draft before relying on or sharing it.

## Example task for the automation

> Every Sunday, prepare a concise U.S. market briefing. Summarize the week's main market and macro developments, list the highest-impact events for the week ahead in America/Chicago time, and identify a few risks and questions to watch. Link each material claim to its source. Use accessible reporting and official data; if a source is unavailable, say so instead of guessing. Keep research ideas separate from facts and do not give personalized buy or sell instructions. Send me a draft for review, not a final decision.

## What you should review

Before using the briefing, check the dates, event times, source links, and any surprising claims. Decide which developments matter to your own situation. The automation saves the repetitive gathering and formatting work; it does not make investment decisions for you.
