## Start with your task

There is no best assistant for every task. The services below change quickly, and features depend on plan, settings, region, and account. These notes describe useful strengths and practical limitations to help you choose what to try; they are not a ranking. Check the provider's current feature and privacy pages before relying on a feature or sharing sensitive material.

| Provider | Where it can be useful | Tradeoffs to keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- |

Deep research can produce a cited report from multiple sources. | Tools and usage limits vary by plan and settings. Citations are leads: open sources and check that they support the claims. ## A quick way to compare

Try the same small, low-stakes task in two assistants. Compare whether each follows your instructions, asks for missing context, shows useful evidence, handles your files well, and makes its limits clear. Then check the plan's current usage limits and data controls. A fluent answer alone is not evidence that the tool is more accurate.
