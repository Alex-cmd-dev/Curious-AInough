# Challenge: Understand Probability Density With AI

[⬅ Back to Examples](README.md)

## The scenario

You are using a continuous probability model for height and learning what its *probability density* means.

## Try the challenge

1. **Start with your own idea.** In this model, which could have positive probability: “exactly 68.1 inches” or “between 68.1 and 68.2 inches”? Write down your guess before using AI.
2. **Ask for coaching.** Try: “Ask me one question at a time to help me understand probability density using height. Give hints before answers.”
3. **Check the explanation.** Ask why one exact value has probability zero in this model while a range can have positive probability. Check the answer against your class notes or a [probability lesson](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat414/Lesson14).
4. **Explain it back.** Close the chat and explain how the *area under a density curve* gives the probability of a range. The curve's height alone is not a probability.
5. **Test and reflect.** Without AI, explain what happens to the probability when a range around 68.1 inches shrinks toward that exact value. Then ask AI to critique your reasoning. What part can you now explain on your own?

## Check your understanding

In a model with a probability density, an exact value has probability zero, but an interval can have positive probability. Some other models assign probability to individual values.
