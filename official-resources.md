# 🔗 Official Resources & Further Reading


*Resource links checked November 4, 2026.* Offers, eligibility, and product details can change; confirm current terms on the linked provider pages.

## How AI works and how to use it

- [OpenAI: Why language models hallucinate](https://openai.com/index/why-language-models-hallucinate/): an explanation of factual errors and uncertainty.
- [OpenAI: A practical guide to building agents](https://openai.com/business/guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-ai-agents/): designing and evaluating agent workflows.
- [Model Context Protocol: Introduction](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/getting-started/intro): the standard for connecting AI applications to external systems.
- [Anthropic: Introducing MCP](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-context-protocol): background on the protocol.
- [OpenAI Academy: Using skills](https://openai.com/academy/skills/): reusable instructions and workflows.
- [OpenAI Academy: Workspace agents](https://openai.com/academy/workspace-agents/): shared agent workflows and access.
- [OpenAI Help: Plugins in ChatGPT and Codex](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001256/): connections and permissions.

## Learning, research, and education

- [UNESCO: AI competency framework for students](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-competency-framework-students): a framework for AI knowledge, use, and ethics.
- [UNESCO: Guidance for generative AI in education and research](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research): human-centered guidance for using generative AI in education and research.
- [OpenAI Academy: Using ChatGPT](https://openai.com/academy/using-chatgpt/): practical learning resources.

- [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot), [JetBrains AI](https://www.jetbrains.com/ai-ides/), [Cursor](https://cursor.com/), and [Google Antigravity](https://antigravity.google/pricing)

## Student offers

- [ChatGPT Back to School offer](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001493-chatgpt-back-to-school-offer-for-students) — eligible U.S. college and graduate students can claim four free monthly billing periods of Plus by October 31, 2026; check eligibility, billing, and renewal terms.
- [Codex for Students](https://developers.openai.com/community/students) — verified university students in the U.S. and Canada can claim $100 in Codex credits; credits expire 12 months after grant.
- [Google AI student offer terms](https://one.google.com/offer/studentoffer8) — eligible students may receive a 12-month offer; terms require verification, payment method, and redemption by December 31, 2026. The included plan varies by region and renews at the local standard price unless canceled.
- [GitHub Education for students](https://github.com/education/students)
- [JetBrains Student Pack](https://www.jetbrains.com/academy/student-pack/) — free education access for eligible students; includes a limited JetBrains AI Pro trial, after which AI features have separate limits or plans. Educational licenses are for non-commercial use.
- [Cursor for students](https://cursor.com/students) — Cursor currently offers free access to start; its student page describes event-based promotions rather than a standing student discount.

> [!NOTE]
> **Reminder:** offers, eligibility, geography, pricing, quotas, and deadlines change. Re-check official links before acting on them. See the [🧰 Tool Directory](11-tool-directory/README.md) for how to evaluate access.
